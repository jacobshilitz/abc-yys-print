The alphabet

The rules every letter follows

  • Small letters go from the bottom line up to the dotted line (in dotted mode they stop just below it, so the dots stay easy to see).
  • Capitals, tall letters and numbers go from the bottom line up to the top line.
  • The bars of A, E, F, H and G, the middles of B, P and R, and where K and Y meet sit on the dotted line (just below it in dotted mode).
  • Tails of g, j, p, q and y go below the bottom line.
  • The small t starts about halfway between the dotted line and the top line, and ends with a small curve.
  • The i and j dot is a short upright dash, a small gap above the dotted line (two dots in dotted mode).
